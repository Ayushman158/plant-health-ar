Care Guide

Epipremnum aureum

Watering

Water only when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry. Money plants tolerate a little drought far better than waterlogging. Ease off in cooler months.

Light

Bright, indirect light. It survives dim corners, but gentle indirect sun brings out richer golden marbling and faster growth.

Humidity

Ordinary indoor humidity (40–60%) is fine. If leaf edges turn dry and crisp, move it away from vents and mist weekly.

Propagation

Cut a vine 1 cm below a leaf node and stand it in clean water or moist mix. Roots usually appear within 10–14 days.
