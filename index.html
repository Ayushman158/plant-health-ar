Money Plant Care Guide

Epipremnum aureum
💧

Watering Schedule

Water only when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels completely dry. Money Plants prefer underwatering over waterlogging. Reduce frequency during cooler seasons.

☀️

Sunlight & Exposure

Thrives in bright, indirect light. Tolerates lower-light indoor corners, though gentle indirect sun promotes richer golden marbling and faster growth.

💨

Humidity & Environment

Flourishes in typical home humidity (40–60%). If leaf edges become dry and crisp, mist the leaves weekly with room-temperature water.

🌱

Propagation

Cut a healthy vine 1 cm below a leaf node and place it in clean water or moist potting mix. Strong root systems typically emerge within 10–14 days.
